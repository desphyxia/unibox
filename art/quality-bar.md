# Art quality bar and weak-parts register

Set by the user on 2026-10-17: the round 5 unicorn (`generators/artgen5.py`, `samples/round5-wings-sleep/`) is the **minimum** level of detail and ambition for every creature and every later round. Nothing new ships below it, and the aim is to go above it.

## The bar (what "round 5 level" means)
- **Built as a rig, not a picture.** A pose is a small set of numbers (body outline, neck curves, limb chains, head placement, hair directions). One drawing routine makes every view.
- **Several poses and views.** At least stand, walk and sleep, plus a front view. Each pose reads as the right action at phone size (320 px wide).
- **Shape traits as layers**, not only colour: wings, mane or tail style, coat pattern, markings. Each can be switched on or off without redrawing the pose.
- **Flowing parts are built from many layered pieces** (locks, feathers, fins), with highlights and depth, never one stiff band.
- **Soft pastel, no outlines.** Shared flat gradient on all coat parts so overlaps merge. Soft shading with radial gradients, tapered crease strokes. No SVG filters.
- **Face with life**: matched eyes with lids, lashes and highlights, blush, a small mouth and nostril. No part outside the face.
- **Checked three ways before it is called done**: in a browser, in Godot's own SVG loader (it differs from browsers), and at 320 px width.
- **Reported honestly**: every round ends with a study page with before and after pictures, a list of what is still weak, and no claim that is not checked.
- **Still no person in the art**: all generated by code or AI.
